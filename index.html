Chess-Sweeper is my take on Minesweeper, played on famous chess positions instead of a random minefield. The pieces are the mines, every number counts the pieces attacking that square, and you win by uncovering every empty square. Mark the squares where you think a piece hides, then find out which classic game or study it was.

Positions and summaries are drawn from Wikipedia's articles on famous games, endgame studies and endgame theory. Nothing leaves your browser.

Provided free of charge, without tracking. For the love of the game.
